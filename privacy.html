Privacy Policy for Flapsy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Flapsy ("the app") is a game published by Martin Nikolaev. This policy explains what data the app handles, who else receives it, and what choices you have.

The app has no accounts, no login, and no server of its own. Your scores, coins, unlocked birds, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and the developer cannot see them.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: high scores, coins, unlocked birds, achievements, daily challenge results
  • The name you type on the start screen, used only for the on-device leaderboard
  • Your purchase status, so a purchase you paid for is not lost

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve, measure and (where permitted) personalise those ads, Google may collect:

  • An advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Your IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Device information such as model, operating system and language
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps

This data is handled by Google as an independent controller under Google's Privacy & Terms. Google's advertising partners are listed in the consent form shown inside the app.

Your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app shows a Google-certified consent form before ads are personalised. You can change your answer at any time with Privacy Options on the app's main menu.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission to track. If you decline, ads still appear but are not personalised.
  • You can reset or clear your advertising identifier in your device settings: iOS under Privacy & Security → Tracking, Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Purchases

Coin packs and Remove Ads are processed entirely by Apple or Google. Payment details never reach the app or the developer — the app is only told whether a purchase succeeded.

Children

Flapsy is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Because the developer holds no personal data about you, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google using the link above. You may also withdraw advertising consent at any time via Privacy Options in the app.
