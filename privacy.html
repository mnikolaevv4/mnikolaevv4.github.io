Privacy Policy for Flapsy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Flapsy ("the app") is a game published by Martin Nikolaev. This policy explains what data the app handles and what choices you have.

The short version

The app collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no accounts, no login, and no server of its own. It shows no advertising, contains no analytics, and makes no network requests at all — it works with the device offline. Everything the app remembers stays on your device, and the developer has no way to see it.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: high scores, coins, unlocked birds, achievements, daily challenge results
  • The name you type on the start screen, used only for the leaderboard on your own device
  • Your settings, such as whether sound is on

None of this leaves the device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising and tracking

There is none. The app shows no ads, uses no advertising identifier (no IDFA on iOS, no Advertising ID on Android), and does not track you across apps or websites. It asks for no tracking permission because it has nothing to track.

Purchases

This version has no in-app purchases. Birds are unlocked with coins earned by playing.

Children

Flapsy is not directed to children under 13. Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. The developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access or erase on our side. The data on your device is removed by deleting the app.

Future versions

A later version may add advertising or in-app purchases. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any consent required in your region will be requested inside the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
